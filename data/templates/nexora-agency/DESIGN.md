## Tone
Premium, assured, results-driven. Numbers and proof points everywhere.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep structure and spacing close to the demo layout (topbar, stats hero, client strip, services, why-us, process, case studies, testimonials, CTA, footer).
- Do stay dark with one indigo accent and subtle card surfaces.
- Don't invent a second palette.
- Don't use real client names; use fictional brands. Photos are limited to the approved Unsplash set below.
