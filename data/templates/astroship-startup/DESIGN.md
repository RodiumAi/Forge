# Design charter

## Template

## Colors
- --bg: #ffffff
- --fg: #0f172a
- --muted: #64748b
- --accent: #4f46e5

## Typography
- Font stack: "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif (system fonts, no web fonts).
- Body: 1rem, line-height 1.6.
- Hero h1: 2.1rem on mobile, 3rem from 761px, line-height 1.15, letter-spacing -0.02em.
- Section h2: 2.1rem (CTA h2 2rem), letter-spacing -0.02em.
- Feature h3: 1.05rem. Body copy: 0.95rem to 1.1rem in --muted.
- Labels: 0.9rem uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em. Logo words: 1.15rem, weight 700.

## Spacing & radius
- Container: max-width 1100px with 24px side padding.
- Section padding: hero 24px top on mobile (56px desktop) and 64px bottom; features 40px 0 72px; CTA 72px bottom; CTA box 64px 32px.
- Gaps: 48px in the hero, 36px 32px in the feature grid, 14px between buttons.
- Radius: 24px hero visual, 20px CTA band, 10px feature icon tile, 8px buttons.
- Breakpoint (mobile first): 761px.

## Tone
Friendly, crisp, confident. Short punchy headlines, generous whitespace.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep structure and spacing close to the demo layout (topbar, split hero, logos, 6-feature grid, dark CTA band, footer).
- Do use a single indigo accent on a white background.
- Don't invent a second palette.
- Don't add heavy imagery beyond the approved photos below; keep the rest as simple stylized shapes.
