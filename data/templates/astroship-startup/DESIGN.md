## Tone
Friendly, crisp, confident. Short punchy headlines, generous whitespace.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep structure and spacing close to the demo layout (topbar, split hero, logos, 6-feature grid, dark CTA band, footer).
- Do use a single indigo accent on a white background.
- Don't invent a second palette.
- Don't add heavy imagery; use simple stylized shapes.
