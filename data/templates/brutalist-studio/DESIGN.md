# Design charter

## Template
- id: brutalist-studio
- name: Raw Works Studio

## Colors
- --bg: #f5f0e8
- --fg: #111111
- --muted: #5c564d
- --accent: #ff4911

Loud secondary hits: #ffe600 (yellow), #1a6dff (blue). Use sparingly on stickers, tags and hovers.

## Typography
- Font stack: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", "Arial Black", Arial, sans-serif (system fonts, no web fonts).
- Body: 1rem, line-height 1.5, antialiased.
- Hero h1: clamp(3rem, 10vw, 8rem), weight 900, line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -0.04em, uppercase.
- Section titles: clamp(2.2rem, 6vw, 4.5rem), weight 900, line-height 0.98, letter-spacing -0.03em, uppercase.
- Shout quote: clamp(1.4rem, 3.6vw, 2.6rem), weight 900. Contact h2: clamp(1.8rem, 5vw, 3.4rem).
- Card titles: 1.1rem to 1.4rem, weight 900, uppercase. Card copy: 0.88rem to 0.95rem.
- Buttons, tags and labels: 0.72rem to 0.92rem, weight 700 to 900, uppercase. Marquee: 1.5rem, weight 900.

## Spacing & radius
- Page gutters: 5vw on every section.
- Section padding: 90px vertical (hero 56px top on mobile, 90px from 641px; FAQ 40px top; contact 100px bottom).
- Grid gaps: 26px services, 24px process, 44px 34px projects; 10px between tags.
- Card padding: 22px to 28px; contact box 64px 5vw.
- Borders: 3px solid #111 (2px on tags and numbers). Shadows: 6px 6px 0 #111, 10px 10px 0 #111 for large blocks.
- Radius: 0 everywhere.
- Breakpoints (mobile first): 641px (nav links, two-column cards) and 961px (four columns, two-column projects, blue sticker).

## Tone
Irreverent, bold, playful, direct. Sentences that punch. No corporate softening, no buzzwords.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep 3px solid black borders and hard offset shadows (box-shadow 6px 6px 0 #000) on every card and button.
- Do keep the giant display typography and the scrolling CSS marquee.
- Do keep rotated sticker badges and clashing color blocks.
- Don't add rounded corners beyond 0-4px, gradients, or soft drop shadows.
- Don't tone down the copy — the attitude is the brand.
