## Colors

- --accent: #ff4911

Loud secondary hits: #ffe600 (yellow), #1a6dff (blue). Use sparingly on stickers, tags and hovers.

## Tone
Irreverent, bold, playful, direct. Sentences that punch. No corporate softening, no buzzwords.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep 3px solid black borders and hard offset shadows (box-shadow 6px 6px 0 #000) on every card and button.
- Do keep the giant display typography and the scrolling CSS marquee.
- Do keep rotated sticker badges and clashing color blocks.
- Don't add rounded corners beyond 0-4px, gradients, or soft drop shadows.
- Don't tone down the copy — the attitude is the brand.
